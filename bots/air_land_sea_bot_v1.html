  • Deal 6 Face Down cards to the Bot (keep them as a draw pile).
  • The remaining 6 cards are out of the game.

Bot Reference

Basics

  • Bot is always the 1st player on every round.
  • Bot plays one from the top of their hand, face up, into a matching theatre–unless the card forces the Bot to withdraw.

Withdrawal

  • If Bot tries to play or move a 4th card into their side of a theatre, they return that card to their hand and withdraw.
  • Avert: Bot may avert withdrawal if a card could negate the condition, observing Bot Priority and Choice.

Bot Priority & Choice

The card being placed/causing an effect is the Reference Card. When given a choice:
  1. Adjacent theatre to 'your' right of Reference.
  2. Adjacent theatre to 'your' left of Reference.
  3. Its own theatre.
  4. Non-adjacent theatre.
  5. If stacked: 1st available card in that stack.

Flip Priority: Their FD to FU > Your FU to FD > Your FD to FU > Their FU to FD.

Bot Priority & Choice

When resolving secondary effects: Bot reads 'may' as 'you must' make the best decision for them.

Full Rules Overview

Blockade: Bot observing a Blockade card limitation will instead place the 'discard' card face down, in an adjacent chosen theatre. Returned cards go to the bottom of the hand.

Secondary Effects: Played after a card has been played or flipped as per box rules. An ineffective, negative or a choice that eventually forces Bot withdrawal must still be played.

Difficulty Enhancements: Do not resolve the Difficulty Enhancement cards' secondary effects. Do not count the Difficulty Enhancement cards toward the Bot withdrawal number.
